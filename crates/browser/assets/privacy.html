Privacy Policy

Echo is a local browser. It has no Echo account or service for synchronizing your browsing data.

Data on this device

Echo stores profiles, Spaces, tabs, settings, browsing history and downloaded-file records locally. Websites can store cookies and other site data in their browser profile. Imported bookmarks, history and tabs are added to the local profile you select. Supported imported passwords and AI credentials use the operating system credential store.

Private windows keep their browsing history and tab state in memory. Their site storage is separate from normal profiles and is discarded when the last private window closes. Downloaded files remain on disk.

Connections you use

Opening websites sends requests to those websites and their services. Searches and search suggestions use your selected search provider. Website sign-in, extensions and services have their own privacy policies.

When you use an AI feature, Echo communicates directly with the provider you configure. Requests can include your prompt and page content needed for that feature. Echo does not operate an intermediary AI server. Review your provider’s privacy policy before sending sensitive information.

Your controls

You can clear browsing history and site data, remove local profiles and revoke website permissions. Removing browser records does not delete downloaded files or data retained by websites, search providers or AI providers.
